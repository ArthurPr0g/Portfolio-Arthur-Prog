Why are your prices lower than US agencies?

I'm an independent developer based in Brazil. A lower cost of living and no agency overhead (offices, account managers, sales teams) let me charge less, using the same modern tools US teams use. Every project in this portfolio was built by me, and many are live for you to check.

How do I know this is safe?

You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and price before paying anything. You pay 50% to start and the other 50% only after you approve the finished project. Your domain is always registered in your name.

How do we work across countries?

Everything is remote: video calls, WhatsApp and email, in English. Brazil is 1–2 hours ahead of US Eastern Time, so we share most of the business day.

What is the monthly plan and why does it exist?

It covers hosting, database, backups, security updates, monitoring and small monthly tweaks. Your website or system stays online, fast and secure, without you worrying about anything technical.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade within 6 months, paying only the difference between the packages.

What's not included?

Business email, payment processor fees (such as Stripe), sales tax setup and paid third-party apps. You provide the texts and photos; on Premium packages I review the copy to help it sell more.

How long does it take?

From 5–10 business days for the Starter website to 28–52 business days for the Advanced system. The timeline starts once I receive all the materials: texts, photos and access.

I already use QuickBooks, Shopify or spreadsheets. Is a custom system worth it?

If off-the-shelf software doesn't fit the way you work, yes. A custom system is built around your process, doesn't charge per extra user and lives inside your own website.
